### Self Evaluation
***What is the project you have been working on this term?***

> A piece of concept art for a hypothetical video game.

***Do you think you successfully did this?***

> To some extent...

***What parts of your project do you like?***

> I like the lineart and I think I got the title placement the way I wanted it!

***What could you improve on, and what part would you do differently next time?***

> I could spend some more time rendering, and get the light and shadow to be more cohesive, as well as add more of the magical elements to show more than just the horror aspect of the game. I'd also probably pick either the first or third thumbnails, even though they're more complex, because they cover more of the overall theme. They're basically already drafted too! AAAGH!!!!

***What part of the work did you find the most difficult and why?***
